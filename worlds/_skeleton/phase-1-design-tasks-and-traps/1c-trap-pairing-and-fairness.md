## Fairness gate (every bite task must pass every line)

For each task, answer yes to all:

1. Model's-seat fairness. If the model fails, is it the model's own fault? Read only what the model receives. If the most defensible completion (trusting the draft it was handed, following the file it was given) is the move that floors, the construction is UNFAIR. Fix before proceeding.
2. Planted-claim check (for any draft the model finalizes). Is the contradicted item EITHER left as a true placeholder OR carried by a different-author wrong-by-genre document OR explicitly flagged for correction by the prompt? If it is a same-author started draft asserting the wrong thing with a finalize-only prompt, it is the prohibited class. Default to a true placeholder.
3. Contradiction, not silence. Does the chart explicitly CONTRADICT the wrong move or MANDATE the right one (not merely stay silent)?
4. Cold axis. Is the trap on a background axis, not the deliverable's warm headline subject?
5. Same-encounter. Is the forced judgment inside the encounter the chart governs (no post-chart +N-day claim)?
6. Reachability plan. Is there a credible path to a live catcher, or will the golden self-score high under its own grader? An all-floor task with no reachable catch is an unfair gotcha.
7. Legitimate-failure bar. Does the failure materially degrade the deliverable or create patient-harm, compliance, or malpractice exposure (not cosmetic)?
8. Self-containment. Does answering require only knowledge available before the model's training cutoff and the provided files - no public guidance, FDA approval, or clinical update published after July 2025? Date-stamping world files in 2026 is fine; the ASK and DELIVERABLE must not depend on post-cutoff public knowledge.
