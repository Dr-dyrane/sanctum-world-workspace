# REFERENCE-MAP - what to open and when

Everything the playground links to, with what each is for. Read DO-NOT-REPEAT and the structure dossier before anything else.

## Read first (cold start)

- `DO-NOT-REPEAT.md` - the concentrated list of every mistake Korvin Merrow made, each written as a rule. State three relevant lines as your no-repeat receipt before building.
- `AGENTS.md` - THE LAW (build it real, difficulty is empirical, fairness is the other half) and the guardrails.
- `docs/task-structure-dossier.md` - the S1-S8 structure taxonomy, the 5+ structures rule, and the autopsy of the sequencing mistake this playground prevents.

## Pod rules to re-confirm each delivery (they change)

- Minimum 30 world-level files (06/10).
- Treat the model as knowing nothing published after July 2025; no real-time web search; avoid tasks that need post-August-2025 public clinical knowledge (FDA approvals, new guidelines). Date-stamping world files in 2026 is fine.
- Three preference labels per task. GA/FA focus on what was done wrong. Self-contained section descriptions (non-negotiables vs allowable variation vs common failure modes).
- A score below 70 percent is not required; a single legitimate critical clinical failure suffices. Latest pod announcements live in `reference/world-spec-guidelines/POD-ANNOUNCEMENT-*`.
