## Handoff Focus

The future medication-safety review should pay attention to:

- HFrEF/CAD medication holds versus appropriate reintroduction;
- renal and potassium safety;
- blood pressure reserve and volume status;
- diabetes medication safety in acute illness and transition;
- inpatient-only insulin actions that must not become outpatient additions by default;
- prednisone history, exposure risk, and taper ambiguity;
- home medication-management reliability and family oversight;
- follow-up monitoring and communication needs.

This file does not select final medication actions.

## Friction Preservation

Cardiology vs Nephrology remains central. Cardiology may emphasize HFrEF/CAD protection and guideline-directed therapy; Nephrology may emphasize renal recovery, potassium risk, hemodynamic reserve, and timing. Both remain careful and defensible.

Endocrinology vs Primary Team remains central for steroid-risk interpretation. Endocrinology does not become the prednisone-history source of truth, and the Primary Team does not become careless for avoiding steroid over-attribution.
