# Korvin Merrow Decision Register

## Already Locked From Brainstorm

- Common hospital medicine complexity, not rare disease puzzle.
- Sepsis is initially reasonable and suspected urinary-source.
- Adrenal insufficiency risk is a competing concern, not the central hidden answer.
- Steroid exposure comes from PMR treated with chronic prednisone and unclear recent taper.
- HFrEF/CAD creates real cardiology involvement.
- CKD stage 3 with AKI/hypotension creates nephrology tension.
- Family baseline knowledge is central to discharge-readiness friction.

## Needs Physician Decision

- Specific outpatient prednisone dose/taper sequence.
- Specific home medication classes and which named agents, if any, should appear in the spec.
- Exact renal, BP, electrolyte, infection, and functional trajectory.
- Specific consultant positions by hospital day.
- Which details are intentionally uncertain versus merely not decided yet.
- Whether race/ethnicity, BMI, occupation, insurance/payer, advance directives, or SDOH details are relevant.
- Naturalistic draft task prompts written by Alexander.

## Source-Of-Truth Rule

- Do not invent labs, dates, medication doses, medication names, imaging results, culture results, allergies, or document filenames.
- If a detail is not provided by Alexander or approved source material, mark it as needs decision.

## Reviewer / AutoQC Risk

- Unsupported specifics will propagate into files and create unintended contradictions.
- Vague clinical logic will make the spec unusable for engineering and reviewers.
- Inconsistent medication or timeline decisions will damage the core trap architecture.
