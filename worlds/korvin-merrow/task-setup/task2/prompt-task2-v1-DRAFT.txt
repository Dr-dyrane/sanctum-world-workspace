KM02 PROMPT - v1 DRAFT (Claude draft for physician edit + ownership; meet-in-the-middle with claude.ai review)

Built to THE LAW: modeled on the KM01 prompt that passed AutoQC (clinician voice, short, scoping in the ask not a checklist). Deliberately does NOT do what locked TP-KM02 does (its bulleted "should reflect" list pre-narrates mixed physiology, copy-forward avoidance, and the prednisone hierarchy = feeding the model the answer). Neutral register: realistic attending ask creates the genre pull toward a closed summary; the chart's silences (no finalized culture, FI-W22 not a final order) do the discriminating. Does not coach against stating a culture, does not say "preserve uncertainty," does not over-press closure beyond a normal attending ask.

OPEN FORK for review (do not resolve in the prompt): run CLEAN (no mounted task file; prompt stands alone, model meets FI-W22 in the chart on its own) vs mount a real clinical "discharge summary request" note. The locked canon lists FI-T02 as a provided context file, but FI-T02 as written is an architecture doc full of trap/meta language and would leak if mounted as-is. v1 below is the clean, stand-alone version.

----- PROMPT BODY (paste this) -----
Korvin Merrow in 5W-318 is set to go home tomorrow, 5/24. I need his discharge summary done today so his PCP and the outpatient team can pick him up without gaps. Work through the chart and write it off the full hospitalization, ED through this morning. Give me the hospital course the way it actually unfolded, his active problems, and where things stand for follow-up.
----- END PROMPT BODY -----

Why each line, against the guardrails:
- "set to go home tomorrow, 5/24 ... discharge summary done today": the realistic genre pressure. A real hospitalist asks exactly this. It is the forcing function we WANT (the genre pulls toward a closed, signed-out document), not artificial over-pressing. This is what makes a fluent model reach for the completing furniture the chart cannot support (a culture result, a final med list, a disposition).
- "Work through the chart ... ED through this morning": points at the full record without a checklist. The model meets the HD1-6 notes, consults, trends, functional sources, and FI-W22 on its own and decides what to do with each. No reasoning handed over.
- "the way it actually unfolded": a normal attending instruction ("tell the story of the admission"). It mildly discourages copy-forward, which is fine because copy-forward is the FAIR ANCHOR a strong model already clears via FI-W22; the prompt does not need to protect it. It does not name sepsis, mixed physiology, or any trap.
- "his active problems, and where things stand for follow-up": standard discharge-summary content. Critically NOT "preserve uncertainty" and NOT "do not finalize" (that would feed the restraint). It is silent on infection/culture, so it does not tip the lead discriminator.

What is deliberately ABSENT (and must stay absent):
- No list of what to reflect (no mixed physiology, no consultant chronology, no prednisone-source hierarchy, no "reconcile FI-W22"). That is the TP-KM02 leak.
- No instruction to preserve openness or avoid a final med list. The genre tension must do this, or the task tests nothing.
- No mention of cultures, organisms, or infection workup. Silence here is the trap.
