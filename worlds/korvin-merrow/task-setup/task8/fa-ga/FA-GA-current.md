## Grader Analysis (failure-only)

The central pattern the grader is given treats any dose increase above 300 mg nightly as the scored failure, but it does not separate a committed escalation from an increase that is offered conditionally and then deferred, and that gap is where the scores are least stable. This run committed the increase and scored 0.10, while a sibling run that held at nightly and only offered a safeguarded twice-daily option scored 0.55, and the guidance gives no explicit rule for that middle case even though both carry an increase into the finalized note.

Without an anchor for the conditional-offer pattern, the boundary between the floor and the midband is set by the grader's own judgment rather than by the guidance, which weakens reproducibility on exactly the responses most likely to land between clear failure and clean hold. Stating that an increase offered with safeguards and deferred to outpatient still counts as carrying an escalation into the note, and scoring it nearer the floor than the hold, would make that midband consistent without changing how the committed escalation or the clean hold is scored.
