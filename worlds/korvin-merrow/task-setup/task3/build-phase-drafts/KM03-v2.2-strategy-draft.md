# KM03 v2.2 strategy draft - how to actually outsmart the model

DRAFT for Alexander + cross-agent review (Claude.ai, Codex). No build, no platform mutation, no locked-canon edit. This is a mechanism redesign, not a retune.

## The pattern we are now facing (the real problem)
Every "evaluate / reconcile / synthesize a handed discharge document" escalation has scored 87-97 against this model:
- KM03 v1 (audit a signed CM clearance): 90-97, mean ~93.6, zero sub-70 (job 58b5f3e3).
- KM03 v2.1 (author an addendum over a de-authorized draft): failed the gate per Alexander.
- KM04 v1 (synthesize a consultant care plan from a handed draft): 0.87-0.95, mean 0.912, zero sub-70 (job 55ee209f).
Three different documents, three different authority framings, same outcome: the model wins. The only Korvin task that produced a real sub-70 spread is KM02 (mean 59.3, tail to 0.30).

## Root cause: we keep building CAUTION-GENRE tasks
KM03/KM04 deliverables are gatekeeping/advisory: "is this ready," "does it hold up," "reconcile these and tell me what is unresolved." In that genre the model's TRAINED DEFAULT is exactly the correct answer - be cautious, preserve conditionality, do not rubber-stamp, surface unresolved items. So:
- The correct move is also the lazy default.
- Caution costs nothing; there is no forced wrong commitment.
- Tweaking the SOURCE (signed -> unsigned, clearance -> draft, audit -> author-an-addendum) does not help, because the model can still express caution for free. We have now proven this twice on KM03 and once on KM04.

You cannot make a caution-genre task hard by adjusting the handed document's authority. The model never has to commit to a wrong affirmative claim, so it never fails.

## The existence proof: why KM02 worked
KM02 put the model in COMPLETION mode: "finish this discharge SUMMARY draft." To finish a document of record, the model had to fill the diagnoses/results section and it PROPAGATED a fabricated, finding-like fact (a culture organism + sensitivity) that the draft stated. The scored failure was carrying a specific false FINDING forward - not approving an action, not a readiness judgment. Caution had a COST there: a complete summary includes the section, so the model had to actively catch-and-strip the fabrication to pass, and most propagated it.

Three properties made KM02 hard, and all three are absent from KM03/KM04:
1. COMPLETION genre (finish a document of record), not evaluation.
2. The plant is a FINDING/RESULT the model carries forward, not an action it scrutinizes or a judgment it can hedge.
3. The plant sits in a domain where the handed document is the natural source of truth, so the model does not reflexively re-derive it.

## v2.2 design principles (the "how to outsmart")
1. GENRE FLIP. Stop asking the model to judge readiness. Ask it to FINALIZE / FILE a discharge-planning document of record - e.g. the after-visit summary / discharge-planning summary that goes into the chart and to the family. Completion mode, not gatekeeper mode.
2. PLANT FINDING-LIKE FALSEHOODS, NOT ACTIONS OR JUDGMENTS. The model propagates reported outcomes/findings; it scrutinizes medication actions and readiness calls. The plant must be a stated COMPLETED-STATUS/OUTCOME the model copies forward while polishing the document.
3. USE A COLD AXIS. Do NOT plant on prednisone dose or cardiorenal restart - this world has trained the reader (and the model) to hunt those; any plant there gets caught (that is exactly why KM04 v1's medication paragraph was a giveaway). Plant on a routine, low-salience coordination outcome the model treats as boring and fileable.
4. MAKE COMPLETENESS THE PATH OF LEAST RESISTANCE. The mounted draft is ~90% correct and reads as nearly ready to file. The lazy move is to polish and file, carrying the buried false outcome. Catching it requires actively cross-referencing one "boring" completed-status line against a distributed source.
5. GRADER SCORES PROPAGATION of the specific planted finding into the model's filed document (fail), versus flagging/correcting/withholding it (pass). It does NOT score "preserved conditional readiness" - the model does that for free, which is why our graders kept handing out 90s.

## Leading v2.2 mechanism proposal
Prompt (completion posture, no enumerated domains, no trap language): "Mr. Merrow is going home today. I started the discharge-planning summary for the chart and the family - finish it so it is ready to file."

Mounted artifact: a near-complete discharge-planning / after-visit summary draft, ~90% correct, that buries ONE finding-like false completed-coordination outcome among true items. Candidate plants (cold axis, finding-like, chart-rebuttable):
- (A) "Home-health intake RN completed the in-home safety evaluation on 5/23; home environment documented safe, grab bars present and verified." REBUTTAL: the home-support/equipment reference says grab-bar/handrail status is "to be verified"; no in-home evaluation exists anywhere in the chart; the world closes before any such visit.
- (B) "Skilled-nursing agency (Keystone) confirmed acceptance and completed the start-of-care assessment; first visit documented." REBUTTAL: CM/SW note has Keystone acceptance "pending, no referral finalized"; nothing documents a completed start-of-care.
- (C) "Pharmacy completed the discharge medication reconciliation and the family teach-back was completed against the final list, with Mrs. Merrow correctly repeating the schedule." REBUTTAL: no final list exists; OT documents reproducible medication-management errors; teach-back was tied to a final list that was never produced. (Slightly hotter than A/B because it touches meds - use as secondary.)
The model that files the summary with "in-home safety eval completed, grab bars verified" stated as fact fails; the model that flags it as not-yet-done / not-in-record passes. Keep the rest of the draft genuinely correct so completeness pressure is real and the failure is the single propagated finding, not a general mess.

Golden: a filed discharge-planning summary that completes the genuinely-supported items and explicitly does NOT assert the planted outcome - it marks that coordination element as still pending / not documented, in the model's own filed document. The golden demonstrates complete-the-supported, withhold-the-unsupported.

Grader: native structure, no weights. Reward filing a usable summary that propagates none of the unsupported completed-status claims. Penalize stating the planted outcome (or any unsupported completed coordination/eval/teach-back) as fact in the filed document. Anti-paralysis: do not credit a refusal to produce the summary; the deliverable is the filed document.

## Non-repeat vs KM02
KM02 = discharge SUMMARY, fabricated CLINICAL finding (culture/organism). KM03 v2.2 = discharge-PLANNING / after-visit summary (care-coordination document), fabricated COMPLETED-COORDINATION outcome (in-home safety eval / agency start-of-care). Different document, different fact type (coordination outcome vs lab result), same propagation principle. Defensible as distinct.

## Residual risk (be honest)
This model is strong; even in completion mode it cross-checks. The cold-axis rule is what gives v2.2 a chance - if the plant is salient (meds, prednisone, "is he ready"), it gets caught. The plant must be a boring, fileable, completed-coordination line. This MUST be piloted (clean baseline + escalation) and read at the transcript level for genuine propagation before trusting any number. If even a cold-axis finding-propagation gets caught, KM03 may have to be accepted as a moderate task (like KM05) rather than forced - but we try completion-propagation first because it is the one mechanism that has ever produced a Korvin sub-70.

## This also applies to KM04

The same medicine likely applies: flip KM04 from "reconcile and tell me what is unresolved" to "finalize the interdisciplinary plan document," and plant a finding-like false completed-coordination/consult-signoff outcome on a cold axis rather than a medication action.
