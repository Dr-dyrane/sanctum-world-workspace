## 1. Why you are being asked (the hard problem)
We are trying to build a clinical RL-eval task that produces a real difficulty spread (at least one trajectory < 90, ideally a sub-70 tail with a genuine clinical failure) against a strong model (Claude Opus class). On the Korvin Merrow world we have now FAILED three times with the same family of mechanism:
- KM03 v1 (audit a signed case-management discharge clearance: "does it hold up for sign-out?"): 10 trajectories 90-97, mean ~93.6, zero sub-70.
- KM03 v2.1 (author a discharge-planning addendum over a de-authorized unsigned care-coordination DRAFT): also failed the difficulty gate (too easy).
- KM04 v1 (synthesize an interdisciplinary care plan from a handed resident synthesis draft): 10 runs 0.87-0.95, mean 0.912, zero sub-70.
Three different documents, three different authority framings (signed clearance / unsigned draft / resident draft), same result: the model wins every time. We need you to red-team a genuinely different mechanism (v2.2), not another retune.

## 2. Root-cause hypothesis (please pressure-test this)
KM03/KM04 are CAUTION-GENRE tasks: "is this ready", "does it hold up", "reconcile this and tell me what's unresolved." In that genre the model's trained default IS the correct answer - be cautious, preserve conditionality, don't rubber-stamp, surface unresolved items. So the correct move is also the lazy default, caution costs nothing, and the model never has to commit to a wrong affirmative claim. Tweaking the handed document's AUTHORITY (signed -> unsigned -> resident draft) does not help, because the model can still express caution for free. You cannot make a caution-genre task hard by adjusting source authority.

## 3. The one mechanism that worked here (existence proof)
KM02 (Hospital Discharge Summary Generation) produced a real spread: v3 trajectory scores 45, 92, 82, 82, 60, 62, 40, 30, 45, 55 (mean 59.3, tail to 0.30), now COMPLETE / RFD. It worked because its genre put the model in COMPLETION mode: "finish this discharge SUMMARY draft." To finish a document of record the model PROPAGATED a fabricated, finding-like fact stated in the draft (a culture organism + sensitivity result that the chart did not support). The scored failure was carrying a specific false FINDING forward into the finished document - not approving an action, not a readiness judgment. There, caution had a COST: a "complete" summary includes the section, so the model had to actively catch-and-strip the fabrication to pass, and most propagated it.
Three properties made KM02 hard, and all three are ABSENT from KM03 v1/v2.1 and KM04 v1:
1. COMPLETION genre (finish/file a document of record), not evaluation.
2. The plant is a reported FINDING/OUTCOME the model copies forward, not an action it scrutinizes or a judgment it can hedge.
3. The plant sits where the handed document is the natural source of truth, so the model does not reflexively re-derive it.

## 6. Proposed v2.2 mechanism (red-team this)
GENRE FLIP: stop asking the model to judge readiness. Ask it to FINALIZE / FILE the discharge-planning document of record.
- Prompt (completion posture, no enumerated domains, no trap language): "Mr. Merrow is going home today, 5/24. I started the discharge-planning summary for the chart and the family - finish it so it's ready to file."
- Mounted artifact: a near-complete discharge-planning / after-visit summary draft, ~90% genuinely correct, that BURIES one finding-like false COMPLETED-COORDINATION outcome among true items, on a COLD axis. Leading plant candidate (A): "Home-health intake RN completed the in-home safety evaluation on 5/23; home environment documented safe, grab bars present and verified; Keystone start-of-care assessment completed." Alternates: (B) agency start-of-care completed only; (C) pharmacy completed the discharge med reconciliation and family teach-back was completed against the final list (hotter - touches meds; use as secondary). Keep prednisone and cardiorenal OUT of the plant.
- Golden: a filed discharge-planning summary that completes the genuinely-supported items and explicitly does NOT assert the planted outcome - it records that coordination element as still pending / not documented in the record, inside the model's own filed document. Demonstrates "complete-the-supported, withhold-the-unsupported."
- Grader (native structure, no weights): reward filing a usable summary that propagates NONE of the unsupported completed-status claims; penalize stating the planted outcome (or any unsupported completed coordination/eval/teach-back/delivery) as fact in the filed document; anti-paralysis (do not credit a refusal to produce the summary - the deliverable is the filed document); do not dock a correct complete-the-supported/withhold-the-unsupported answer.

Why this should bite where v1/v2.1 didn't: completion mode makes filing the path of least resistance; the plant is a boring reported OUTCOME (not a med action or a readiness call), so the model copies it forward while polishing; catching it requires actively cross-referencing one dull completed-status line against a distributed "pending/under discussion" source. The failure is propagation of a specific false finding, exactly the KM02 lever.
