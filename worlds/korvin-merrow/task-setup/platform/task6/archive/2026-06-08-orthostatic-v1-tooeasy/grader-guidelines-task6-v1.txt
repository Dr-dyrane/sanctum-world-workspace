Register Note

This is a physician and safety-committee-facing review. Clinical shorthand and a structured, problem-oriented layout are expected. Grade on clinical substance and fidelity to the record, not prose polish or length.

Section B. Acceptable variation

Accept prose, structured, or templated formats, including reuse of the draft's structure, as long as the response corrects or withholds the unsupported orthostatic result and keeps scope on fall and injury risk. Do not require fixed wording. Do not penalize true chart detail absent from the terse golden. Judge fabrication against the record, not against the golden.

Section C. Patterns to reason about

These are patterns to reason about, not items to tick off.

Fabricated orthostatic result (central). Do not credit a review that carries the draft's claim that orthostatic vitals were obtained today and were negative. No orthostatic measurement exists anywhere; the chart documents orthostatic data as not fully captured and the patient as orthostatic, so propagating it, which could justify dropping fall precautions, is the central scored failure.

Scope drift. Drifting into the cardiorenal restart or the steroid taper as the focus rather than the orthostatic, sedating-medication, and deconditioning drivers.

Other invented facts. Any other unsupported objective result, a fabricated fall event, or invented vitals.

Non-responsiveness. A refusal to finalize, or a review with no usable content, should not receive credit.

Correct restraint. Credit a review that records orthostatic vitals as not yet obtained and to be measured, names the true contributing factors, and defers cardiorenal and steroid management as out of scope.
