## Current Locked Burden

- Polypharmacy/medication complexity

## Recommended Additions For Approval

### 1. Hyperlipidemia

Rationale:

- Highly plausible with CAD, diabetes, and hypertension.

Interaction with existing world:

- Supports high-intensity statin use and long-term cardiovascular prevention.

Impact on traps/frictions/tasks:

- Adds medication-reconciliation specificity without changing the core case.
- Strengthens discharge medication review and CAD/HFrEF continuity reasoning.

Recommendation:

- Approve.

### 2. Anemia of CKD

Rationale:

- Common in CKD stage 3 and older medically complex patients.

Interaction with existing world:

- Can contribute to weakness/fatigue without becoming the central explanation.

Impact on traps/frictions/tasks:

- Supports the broader "not all weakness is sepsis" reasoning.
- Adds lab-trend interpretation complexity but should remain secondary.

Recommendation:

- Approve if we keep it mild/chronic and avoid making it a new diagnostic puzzle.

### 3. Osteoporosis / osteopenia from chronic glucocorticoid exposure

Rationale:

- Plausible with chronic prednisone for PMR.

Interaction with existing world:

- Reinforces that unnecessary steroid continuation has real downside.

Impact on traps/frictions/tasks:

- Strengthens Endocrinology/Hospital Medicine steroid-risk framing.
- Adds medication list complexity if on alendronate, calcium, or vitamin D.

Recommendation:

- Approve.

### 4. Obstructive sleep apnea

Rationale:

- Common in a 62-year-old cardiometabolic patient and relevant to inpatient cognition/fatigue.

Interaction with existing world:

- Could contribute to daytime somnolence, delirium vulnerability, or discharge planning if CPAP adherence is inconsistent.

Impact on traps/frictions/tasks:

- Adds realistic functional/cognitive ambiguity without changing the core sepsis/AKI/steroid/medication arc.

Recommendation:

- Approve only if it remains background complexity and does not become a major trap.

### 5. Diabetic peripheral neuropathy

Rationale:

- Plausible in long-standing diabetes.

Interaction with existing world:

- Supports baseline gait vulnerability and fall risk.

Impact on traps/frictions/tasks:

- Strengthens family/PT discharge-safety friction.
- Helps explain why "weaker than baseline" matters in real-world function.

Recommendation:

- Approve.
