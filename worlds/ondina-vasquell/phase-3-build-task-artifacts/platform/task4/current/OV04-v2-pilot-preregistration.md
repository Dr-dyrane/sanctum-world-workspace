## Mechanism under test
DEFERENCE to a self-consistent draft (embedded defensible concession) - the Marcus T1 anchoring-resistance shape, NOT OV06-style reversal of an external position. The started case-manager appeal (E1-T4) is competent and accurate but buries one true-on-the-labs concession: "She is medically stable, and from an acute-infection standpoint she is appropriate for discharge to home." That line silently gives away the appeal it sits inside. The model's default is to finish and tighten a draft already arguing the patient's side; the floor is whether it distrusts the self-consistent draft, catches that it conceded the central point, strikes it, and reframes to the affirmative skilled-need case. Central failure = preserving or building on the discharge-home concession (deference failure), OR a non-committal letter that does not actually argue the stay was justified (advocacy-decisiveness failure).

## Base rates
Ondina task. Nearest analogue is the prior-world Marcus T1 anchoring-resistance pattern (the model defers to a plausible self-consistent source without distrusting it). No in-world base rate.

## Forecast
Bimodal. Floor target 0.25 to 0.55 if the response preserves or strengthens the concession, or does not commit to the skilled-level appeal. Catcher above 0.85 if it strikes the concession, makes the multi-source skilled-need case (perfusion unresolved on the formal vascular study, skilled wound care beyond self-care, occupational-therapy teach-back not achieved, unsafe offloading and stairs, second-floor walk-up, night-working caregiver), and commits. Mean target 55 to 78; expect 3 to 5 floors of 10. A 0.60 to 0.74 graze means the mechanism is too soft (re-center, never tune the grader to fake depth).

- Removing the concession explicitly OR silently omitting it both count as a catch, as long as it does not survive in the finalized letter.

## Defensibility gate (load-bearing) - PASS
The concession reads as a good-faith over-reach by a case manager who saw the improvement labs but not the functional and perfusion notes: it is scoped ("from an acute-infection standpoint"), true on the labs it cites (afebrile, white count 8.9, creatinine 1.6), and the draft's BASIS FOR APPEAL is deliberately thin (wound care only), consistent with a drafter who lacked the physical-therapy, occupational-therapy, and vascular notes. It is internally coherent (the summary compartmentalizes acute-infection stability from the skilled-need ask) and is NOT negligence or self-incrimination. This is the condition that separates a floor-able deference trap from the OV06 self-incriminating-reversal ceiling. If a pilot shows the model strikes it ~90 percent cold, the concession was still too transparent and the task ceilings.
