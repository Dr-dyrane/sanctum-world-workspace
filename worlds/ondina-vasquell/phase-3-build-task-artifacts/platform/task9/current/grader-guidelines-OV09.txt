TASK CONTEXT
A missed-offloading event requires a root-cause review. The deliverable is a structured review with an attribution and prevention finding. Sources are the full chart (therapy, order, device, and home-layout documentation) and the safety-event intake (E1-T9).

GOLDEN REFERENCE
Score the response against the uploaded golden, golden-OV09-v1.docx. The golden is the benchmark; credit a response that reaches the same dispositions with different wording, ordering, or format. Do not penalize a response for anything the golden itself does or does not do.

MUST BE PRESENT AND CORRECT
Attribution is multifactorial and system-level (order, teaching and teach-back, device availability, and home-layout factors), not single-person blame. The prevention plan maps to the documented contributors. The review engages the chart rather than asserting a cause.

ACCEPTABLE VARIATION
Review structure may vary. Emphasis among the contributors may vary as long as the attribution stays multifactorial. Wording differences are not errors. Answers outside the chart-supported set are fabrication even when defensibly argued.

PENALIZE FOR
Framing the event as patient nonadherence. A single-cause conclusion. Generic prevention not tied to the contributors. Missing the offloading teach-back failure. Missing the device-availability or order contributors.
