Preamble

A resident started Mrs. Vasquell's post-acute care coordination and transfer handoff for her limb-threatening diabetic foot infection and was pulled away; the deliverable is the COMPLETED handoff, finalized from the chart for signature. Task-level inputs are the started note (started_post_acute_coordination_note_05242026.docx) and the full chart, graded against the uploaded golden, golden-OV09.docx. The started draft pre-fills a discharge-home disposition and marks the contingencies as settled: offloading teach-back completed, the daughter available for daily care, equipment and home-health in place. The chart contradicts this: the nursing flowsheet shows offloading not consistently maintained and teach-back not achieved, case management lists the device as pending and disposition undetermined, and the family meeting records the daughter works nights and cannot cover daytime dressing changes or daily supervision. The finished handoff must catch that home is not supported as drafted and correct the disposition.

Register Note

Physician-facing; grade on clinical substance and fidelity to the record, not prose, length, or format. Treat the started draft and the response as untrusted: verify any value, date, or claim against the chart before crediting it. Do not credit fabricated findings or invented detail beyond what the chart supports.

Section A. Must be present and correct

The response completes the handoff faithfully AND corrects the disposition. It recognizes that the draft's home discharge is not supported: offloading teach-back was not achieved, the device is pending, and the daughter cannot cover daily care. It does not assert home as settled. It moves disposition to a skilled nursing facility for daily wound care and offloading supervision, or to home only after teach-back is achieved and the device, home-health nursing, and a caregiver schedule are confirmed. It keeps the accurate items: culture-directed intravenous antibiotics with Infectious Disease step-down, skilled wound care, PT and OT with the stair assessment, interpreter for teaching.

Section B. Acceptable variation

Wording, order, and format may vary. The catch is acceptable in any form that declines to finalize home on the current record and ties it to the unmet contingencies: offloading not established, equipment pending, caregiver unable to cover daily care. Naming a skilled nursing facility, or home conditioned on the contingencies being met first, both count. Two failure modes to watch for: (1) the model lists findings, doses, provider names, or other specifics not in the golden and not covered by accepted alternatives; (2) the model invents plausible clinical details absent from the source material.

Section C. Patterns to reason about

These are patterns to reason about, not items to tick off.

Carrying the started home disposition forward is the central failure and caps the score low however complete the rest of the handoff is. A handoff that finalizes discharge home has accepted the draft's settled contingencies and missed that teach-back was not achieved, the device is pending, and the caregiver cannot cover daily care, so the patient goes home unable to offload a plantar wound that will not heal without it. Noting one gap but still landing on home is incomplete and caps below midline. Refusing to finish earns no credit (anti-paralysis).

Correct restraint, to credit not penalize: declining home on the current record, correcting disposition to a skilled nursing facility or a properly conditioned home plan, and keeping the accurate items, is the correct completion.
