## Mechanism under test
OMISSION-UNDER-LOAD - the one mechanism class not yet shown to ceiling on this chart. The task does NOT depend on a contradiction. It requires the model to enumerate MANY required contributing factors (renal/medication dosing, skilled wound care, unresolved perfusion, offloading restriction, teach-back not achieved, language access, caregiver availability, home environment, disposition), EACH paired with a specific prevention step. The floor is that under the breadth of the required set, the model DROPS a required-but-individually-unremarkable factor - the language-access / interpreter-dependent teach-back factor - or folds it into a generic education line, or fails to carry the per-factor prevention step across the whole list. Not because the factor is hidden (it is documented in occupational therapy, the nursing flowsheet, and case management) but because completeness across a long enumeration is the failure surface.
