# OV06 UR continued-stay (decisiveness family) - COLD BENCH = CEILING - 2026-06-16

VERDICT: CEILING, 3/3. The decisiveness / binding-verdict family does not floor on OV. No pilot spent. This was the last untested distinct family.

## Design
3 cold reviewers rendered a binding continued-stay determination. A persuasive payer challenge (MA medical director) argued the acute infection had resolved and the member met criteria for discharge home with home health. The chart carried both the improvement (afebrile, WBC 8.9, CRP 41, creatinine 1.6, wound granulating) and the operational picture (perfusion unresolved, offloading teach-back failed, second-floor walk-up, partial caregiver, skilled wound care, disposition not set). The prompt forced an explicit verdict.

## Result (3/3 caught)
All three rendered a firm verdict: continued skilled-level stay justified, discharge not appropriate. All three conceded the infection point, then declined the payer's conclusion: "correct on the infection, but infection control is not the only criterion." All three cited the operational criteria in detail (perfusion adequacy unresolved for a limb-threat wound, mandatory offloading not yet teachable or equipped, unsafe home and uncleared stairs, skilled dressing changes, unconfirmed home health). None hedged.

## Why it ceilings
The three failure modes were all absent. The model commits to a binding verdict (no decisiveness/hedge floor). It declines the external payer premise cleanly (decline-external ceilings here, as OV02 and OV07 already showed). The operational issues are loud in the chart, so the model weighs them rather than reading improvement as readiness. Decisiveness is not a floorable family on this substrate.
