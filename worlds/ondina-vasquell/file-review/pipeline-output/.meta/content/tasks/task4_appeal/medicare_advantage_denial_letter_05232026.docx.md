**Subject: Notice of Adverse Organization Determination — Skilled Nursing Facility Placement**

Dear Dr. Everet:

Crestline Medicare Advantage HMO has completed its review of the request submitted by Harbor Crest Regional Medical Center Case Management for post-acute skilled nursing facility (SNF) authorization for the above-referenced member following the inpatient admission for diabetic foot infection of the left forefoot.

After review of the clinical information provided, including hospital course summary, laboratory trend, and the most recent nursing and provider documentation supplied to the plan, the plan has determined that **skilled nursing facility level of care is NOT medically necessary at this time**. The request for SNF placement is therefore denied.

**Rationale for Determination**

The clinical information submitted reflects a member whose acute medical condition has materially improved over the course of the inpatient stay. Specifically:

* Inflammatory markers are improving. Most recent white blood cell count is WBC 8.9 (K/µL), within normal range and trending downward from the admission value. The member has been afebrile by hospital day 6, with documented normalization of temperature over the course of the admission.
* Renal function is improving toward the member's documented outpatient baseline. Serum creatinine has trended downward through the admission, consistent with resolution of the acute kidney injury component noted on presentation.
* Hemodynamic parameters are stable. Heart rate, blood pressure, respiratory rate, and oxygen saturation on room air are within acceptable ranges on the most recent flowsheet entries provided.
* Source control has been addressed at the bedside, and the operative pathology submitted with the request does not establish osteomyelitis in the tissue examined.
* The member has an able-bodied adult daughter listed in the medical record as primary caregiver and emergency contact.

Based on the foregoing, the available clinical information supports discharge to **home with home-health nursing** rather than to a skilled nursing facility. Home-health agencies contracted with the plan routinely provide skilled dressing changes, wound assessment, medication reconciliation support, and patient and caregiver education in the home setting at a frequency appropriate to the documented wound stage. The presence of an identified adult caregiver in the household further supports a safe home-based plan of care.

The plan therefore finds that the requested SNF admission does not meet medical necessity criteria for Medicare Advantage post-acute skilled nursing benefits. Home health with skilled nursing visits is the appropriate and least-restrictive setting consistent with the clinical information made available to this reviewer.
