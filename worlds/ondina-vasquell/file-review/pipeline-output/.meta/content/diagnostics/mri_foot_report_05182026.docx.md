## FINDINGS

**Skin and plantar soft tissues:** Plantar soft-tissue ulceration over the first to second metatarsal head region with adjacent soft tissue edema, fat-stranding, and avid enhancement on the post-contrast sequences. Findings are consistent with overlying cellulitis and active soft-tissue infection contiguous with the ulcer base.

**Deep soft-tissue compartments:** No rim-enhancing fluid collection, no drainable abscess, and no sinus tract extending to a discrete fluid pocket are identified. Intrinsic plantar musculature demonstrates mild adjacent reactive edema without focal necrosis or myositis with frank collection.

**Osseous structures — forefoot:** Bone marrow edema is present in the adjacent first and second metatarsal heads, manifest as hyperintense signal on fluid-sensitive sequences with corresponding low-to-intermediate T1 signal in the subchondral marrow and post-contrast enhancement. No cortical destruction, no cortical breach, no periosteal reaction, and no sequestrum is identified. Marrow signal changes are nonspecific and may reflect reactive marrow change adjacent to the contiguous soft-tissue infection.

**Osseous structures — midfoot and hindfoot:** Tarsal bones, talus, calcaneus, and navicular demonstrate normal marrow signal. No additional foci of marrow edema. No occult fracture.

**Joints:** First metatarsophalangeal joint without effusion or synovial enhancement to suggest septic arthritis. Lesser metatarsophalangeal joints likewise without abnormal effusion or synovial enhancement.

**Tendons, ligaments, plantar fascia:** Flexor and extensor tendons intact without tenosynovitis. Plantar fascia intact. No tendon sheath fluid collection.

**Neurovascular:** No discrete intramuscular or subcutaneous venous thrombus or focal vascular abnormality on the limited non-vascular sequences. Vascular calcifications noted incidentally, consistent with known peripheral arterial disease.

**Other:** Subcutaneous edema overlying the dorsum and plantar forefoot. No foreign body. No subcutaneous gas. No drainable abscess. No cortical destruction.

---

## IMPRESSION

1. Plantar forefoot soft-tissue infection / cellulitis over the first to second metatarsal head region, contiguous with the plantar ulcer.

2. Bone marrow edema in the adjacent first and second metatarsal heads. Early osteomyelitis cannot be excluded; findings are equivocal and not diagnostic alone. The marrow signal abnormalities may represent reactive change adjacent to the contiguous soft-tissue infection rather than true osseous infection. Recommend correlation with clinical course, inflammatory marker trend, deep-tissue culture and surgical pathology results, and treating-team judgment.

3. No cortical destruction, no sequestrum, and no drainable abscess identified.
