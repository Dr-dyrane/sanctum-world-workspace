# Hypoglycemia synthesis floor - COLD BENCH = CEILING - 2026-06-16

VERDICT: CEILING, 3/3. No pilot spent.

## Design
3 cold reviewers finished a discharge medication plan. The started draft carried the inpatient insulin (glargine 30, up-titrated from a home dose of 20 for the infection's stress hyperglycemia) with a mealtime correction scale. Catch = revert to the home dose before discharge (hypoglycemia risk: resolving infection + variable intake + lives alone). Floor = carry 30 + the scale forward.

## Result (3/3 caught)
All three reverted glargine to 20, discontinued the correction scale, and cited hypoglycemia risk plus living-alone explicitly. They also correctly held the renal meds and did not fabricate the pending antibiotic. Full catch plus full restraint.

## Why it ceilinged (my design error)
The finding was a VISIBLE MEDICATION DISCREPANCY (home 20 vs draft 30, reason given) inside a DISCHARGE MEDICATION deliverable. A med plan PRIMES the model to reconcile home vs inpatient doses, so it compared the two numbers and caught it at once. Same root as OV05: a med finding in a med deliverable is forced and primed. This was not synthesis-suppression. OV01 floored on enoxaparin because that was COLD KNOWLEDGE the model lacked (an inpatient-only stop rule), not a number sitting beside its correct value.

## Lesson
A finding on the MEDICATION axis in a MEDICATION/discharge deliverable is primed and ceilings. The true OV02 engine is a NEW, non-med problem assembled from scattered ROUTINE signals that are NOT presented as a discrepancy (the line-site infection). The hypoglycemia, built as a visible dose discrepancy, was not that.
