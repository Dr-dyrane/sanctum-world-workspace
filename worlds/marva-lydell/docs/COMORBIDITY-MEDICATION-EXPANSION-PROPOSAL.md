## The lessons being applied
- KM rule (comorbidity + medication expansion packages): every condition and every drug must ARM a friction, a trap, an un-primed axis, or baseline realism. Never add to pad a count. KM's own tables carry "supports frictions / supports traps" columns. We do the same below.
- KM rule: do not let an addition answer an open clinical question or become a new dominant arc. Keep mixed physiology.
- OV floor rule (FLOOR-MECHANISM-LIBRARY): floors live on UN-PRIMED axes the chart is SILENT on, never on axes the chart flags open (those are primed and ceiling). So the over-closure bone-health axis stays silent; anemia and PAD stay stable background, never flagged "open/unresolved."
- OV proven floors to arm here: the held-med resume under a completion frame (metformin / SGLT2i / ACE-or-ARNI held in AKI, OV09 v4); the discharge-insulin de-escalation (stop the home sliding scale, OV03); the CKD-MBD bone-health un-primed over-closure axis (on cholecalciferol, no DEXA, OV section 5).
- W3 quiet-baseline-trap: anchor a documented baseline (baseline creatinine/eGFR, dry weight, home oxygen status, baseline hemoglobin, last/none DEXA) the model must use instead of the admission or discharge value.

## Two OV guardrails to hold while building
- KEEP QUIET: anemia and PAD status must read as stable background, never flagged open or unresolved. A flagged-open axis is primed and ceilings; the over-closure floor must ride the silent bone-health/CKD-MBD axis.
- Baseline anchors: give the chart a documented baseline creatinine/eGFR, dry weight, home oxygen status, baseline hemoglobin, and "no recent DEXA" so the model must use the baseline rather than the admission/discharge value (the W3 quiet-baseline trap).
