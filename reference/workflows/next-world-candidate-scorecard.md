## The dimension the proposal under-weighted: cross-world trap redundancy

Abi reviews both worlds, and the client sees the suite as one body of work. KM has now SPENT these headline traps: wound-photo miss on a diabetic plantar lesion (KM08 v7), sepsis-to-principal anchoring (KM09), encephalopathy CDI overreach (KM10), unverified home-readings restart (KM05, KM06), held-med soft closure (KM07). Rule for World #2: structures may repeat freely (they are the program's grammar); headline TRAPS may not. Apply that and the field reorders:

- Candidate 2 (stone urosepsis) takes the biggest hit: urinary-source sepsis, AKI on CKD, restart timing, and sepsis coding are all KM axes. A reviewer reads it as Korvin Merrow with a stent. HOLD.
- Candidate 7 (cirrhosis) is physiologically the freshest, but its most tempting CDI axis (encephalopathy severity) is exactly KM10's trap. Choosable only if the CDI/coding axes center elsewhere (hepatorenal vs prerenal POA, SBP criteria, malnutrition severity with the KM10 item-3 echo also avoided).
- Candidate 1 (DFI) has ONE spent element: the wound photo as a scored miss. Mitigation is structural, not cosmetic: in World #2 the wound photo is SUBSTRATE (evidence the tasks consume), never again the headline trap. The world leads with vascular, ID-dosing, coding-specificity, payer, and quality axes that KM never touched.

## Recommendation

PRIMARY: Candidate 1, diabetic foot infection with limb threat, with the photo-demotion mitigation above. It is the only candidate scoring 2 on all ten criteria, it has the CDC amputation anchor, the richest stakeholder lattice (hospitalist, podiatry, vascular surgery, ID, endocrine, wound care nursing, PT/OT, case management, CDI, HIM, payer, family), and its trap families rotate cleanly away from KM: renal antibiotic dosing, contrast-on-CKD, offloading and DME logistics, osteomyelitis specificity, pressure-injury versus diabetic-ulcer coding, measure lookback logic.

RUNNER-UP: Candidate 7 (cirrhosis) if maximal physiologic freshness is wanted and the encephalopathy-CDI axis is excluded by design. SAFE CLASSIC: Candidate 3 (HF multimorbidity), with the note that its restart and diuresis axes brush KM05. BOLD OPTION: Candidate 10 scores high and its off-text evidence (handwritten CIWA sheets, photographed bottles) is excellent, but capacity and SMI portrayal raise reviewer-sensitivity and grader-subjectivity risk (C8); choose it deliberately, not by default. HOLD: Candidate 2 (redundancy), Candidate 9 (specialist drift, weakest structure variety).
