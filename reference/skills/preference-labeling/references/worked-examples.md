# Worked examples

Five labeled pairs, each showing the central failure, the framing, the decider, and
why the tier landed where it did. Use them to calibrate when a pair feels borderline.

## 1. Both-floor severity, plain B (KM10 PL1)

Central failure: confirm an unsupported metabolic encephalopathy the treating team
never diagnosed. Golden declines it.

Pair: A 0.15 vs B 0.20. Both confirm the diagnosis and file chart-ready,
present-on-admission language for it. Both floor.

Decider: degree of commitment to the same wrong determination. A argues the
diagnosis is the most specific correct label and ranks it above the query's safer
options; B confirms but stacks explicit exclusions and hedges.

Tier: B1, plain B. Both-floor severity comparison, decided on degree, so tier 1.
Not B2, because B is not a materially safer clinical result; both file the same
harmful determination. The lesson: when both fall for the trap, the margin is small
and you must say neither is a catch.

## 2. A avoids the unsafe closure, A+ (KM07 PL1)

Central failure: close a held bone-health agent (alendronate) as continued therapy
instead of leaving it open for nephrology.

Pair: A 0.62 vs B 0.40. B writes that alendronate "continues on its Sunday
schedule," the exact unsafe closure. A does not make that visible closure but also
does not affirmatively route alendronate to nephrology.

Decider: B commits the central unsafe closure; A avoids it.

Tier: A2, A+. A avoids the discrete error B makes, so one plus. Not A++, because A
does not itself make the clean golden move (it may simply omit the item rather than
route it). One side avoiding the trap while not fully catching it is the classic A+.

## 3. Discrete provenance error, A+ (OV01 PL1)

Central failure: discontinue the carried-forward inpatient enoxaparin at discharge
(inpatient-only prophylaxis, bleeding risk on aspirin and clopidogrel).

Pair: A 0.90 vs B 0.72. Neither discontinues it; both defer to confirmation. B
calls enoxaparin "New (not on MAR/home list)," a factual provenance error on the
exact item the task targets. A treats it as a carried-forward order.

Decider: B's discrete provenance error, which A avoids.

Tier: A2, A+. A discrete error one side makes earns one plus. Not A++, because
neither affirmatively discontinues the agent, so neither catches the trap; the cap
rule holds the margin at one plus.

## 4. Degree only, plain A (OV01 PL2)

Same central failure as example 3.

Pair: A 0.90 vs B 0.85. B actually names the golden's principle, that enoxaparin is
"prophylaxis, not a home med," but still lands on "Sign with conditions" and calls
the rationale "sound." A does not name the principle but reaches a marginally more
cautious verdict ("confirm it is still indicated, give a stop criterion").

Decider: A's verdict is marginally less endorsing of continuation. There is no
discrete error, B's insight is correct and to its credit, and the gap is 0.05.

Tier: A1, plain A. A degree-only difference with no discrete error stays plain, and
the tiny grader gap agrees. Crediting B's correct principle while still preferring A
is the point: the decider is the outcome on the central item, not the phrasing.

## 5. Loser endorses the trap and floors, A+ (OV01 PL3)

Same central failure as example 3.

Pair: A 0.55 vs B 0.45. A recognizes enoxaparin is "inpatient VTE prophylaxis, not
a home med" and treats it as a change with an SNF reassessment. B calls the same
order "a good call clinically" and groups it under "confirm, then likely sign,"
endorsing continuation without recognizing it is inpatient-only, and floors.

Decider: B endorses the inpatient-only prophylaxis; A is the more cautious,
principle-aware handling. (B earns a real but non-central point for producing a
Spanish instruction sheet; it does not touch the central item.)

Tier: A2, A+. B commits the central-item error A avoids, so one plus. Not A++,
because A still calls the prophylaxis "likely appropriate" rather than discontinuing
it, so it is not a clean catch. Not plain A, because the gap is clear (0.10) and B
clearly endorses the trap while A names the principle.
