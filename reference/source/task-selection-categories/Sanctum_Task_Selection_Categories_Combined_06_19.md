## **How to use this list**

This document lists the workflows currently approved for task creation. Each row gives the workflow name, a short description of what the workflow involves, and the work product you are expected to produce.

Workflows are grouped by priority tier. As a general rule, P0 is the highest priority for coverage, followed by P1 and then P2. The list runs in that order: all P0 workflows first, then P1, then P2.

## **Workflows that may look out of scope but are acceptable**

Some workflows on this list may not look, at first glance, like work a physician normally does. That is acceptable. A workflow can be used as long as the final work product is something a physician would either produce directly or review and sign off on while acting in a medical director or healthcare administrator role.

For example, Inpatient Pharmacotherapy Management can sound like a pharmacist’s responsibility. It can still be used here, provided the task is set up so the physician is communicating or working with the pharmacist and the final work product reflects input from both.

The one requirement that cannot be relaxed is that the voice and point of view of the final work product must be the physician’s. For example, a suitable prompt would be: *“Draft a response to the pharmacist explaining why vancomycin would be inappropriate for this patient, citing the latest IDSA guidelines as of 2025.”*
